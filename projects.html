Finding steel in the paperwork

A piece mark can appear in a model, an advance material list, and delivery paperwork. Seeing its location and fabrication lot in one view makes those records easier to reconcile.

The source archive contains 467 files, including 405 PDFs and 41 workbooks. That includes revisions and derived files. Separately, the archived steel model contains 23,179 assemblies across 13,876 marks.

Locate the piece

Start with a mark, then find its model location and lot instead of searching drawings and lists separately.

Check its status

Keep detailing, fabrication, shipping, and receiving distinct. A shipping record alone does not establish receipt on site.

Follow the evidence

Use the source records to resolve holds, missing delivery evidence, and discrepancies before the next coordination conversation.

Source archive inventoried November 1, 2026. Archive file counts and model quantities describe different datasets; they are not a count of files visualized. The benefit is less repeated lookup and reconciliation; time and cost savings have not been measured.

The evolution

Platform development

Early pour maps, tool tracking, embed workflows, and schedules each solved a narrow problem. OpenYap brings that work into a connected workspace.

The point is practical: fewer handoffs, clearer context, and information that helps the next person do their job.

Beyond the code

Implementation and evaluation

I lead discussions with internal leadership, IT, Data, and Cybersecurity about scaling construction tools company-wide.

I also led the evaluation of an external AI vendor and recommended against adoption, saving $75,000. Good judgment includes knowing when another tool is not the answer.

Through Provectus AI consultation, I advise contractors with $2B–$6B in revenue on labor efficiency, inventory procurement, and immediate return on investment.
